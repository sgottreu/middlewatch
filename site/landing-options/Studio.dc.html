About the studio

Middle Watch makes things for the quiet parts of the day: stories to listen to, sound to settle into, and soon, things to read and play.

It's a small studio that makes stories, sound and games using modern tools, including AI, with a lot of human direction. New formats will join the index as they're ready. To collaborate, license audio or just say hello, email is the best way in.

The name comes from the midnight-to-four watch at sea, the quiet shift.
