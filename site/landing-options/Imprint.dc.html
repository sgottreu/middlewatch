About the studio

Middle Watch makes things for the quiet parts of the day: stories with a beginning, a middle and an end, and sound you can leave on for hours.

It's a small studio that makes stories, sound and games using modern tools, including AI, with a lot of human direction.

The name comes from the midnight-to-four watch at sea, when most of the world is asleep.
