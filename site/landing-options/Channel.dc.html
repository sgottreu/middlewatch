Made for the quiet parts of the day.

Middle Watch is a small studio that makes stories, sound and games using modern tools, including AI, with a lot of human direction.

Each format gets its own channel, so you can follow the one you want. The first episodes are in production now, and things to read and play are on the way.

The name is borrowed from the midnight-to-4am watch at sea.
