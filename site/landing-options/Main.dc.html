Entry 01 Watch: Stories

Stories, read aloud

Original short fiction, narrated. Grounded in history and often lit with a Regency hand: candle smoke, letters sealed with wax, roads that take a week. Each story stands on its own; some quietly join others across four-part arcs. Cozy science fiction and fantasy after the fall are on the chart for later voyages.

Entry 02 Watch: Ambience

Ambience, kept long

Hours of sound to read by, work by or sleep under: candlelight in a still room, snow against the glass, rain on slate, the sea turning over at night. Some are the rooms and shores the stories live in. Others are simply somewhere quiet to be.

Why the name

Midnight to four, while the ship sleeps.

Aboard a sailing ship the night is cut into watches. The middle watch runs from midnight to four in the morning, the long dark stretch when the ship is asleep and one hand stays up to mind the lamp and listen to the bell.

That is when this work gets made, and it is the hour it is made for: for whoever is still awake, or wants help not to be. A story to keep you company. A sound to keep the room.

The bell rings each half hour. Eight bells and the watch is kept.
