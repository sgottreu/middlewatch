# Middle Watch messaging guide

Decided November 2026 while redesigning the middlewatch.co landing page.
Reference designs: `site/landing-options/` (E, F and H use this copy).

## Structure: Listen / Read / Play

Everything Middle Watch makes is grouped by what you do with it, not by format or by world.

| Section | Holds | On the site now? |
|---|---|---|
| Listen | Audio stories, ambience | Yes |
| Read | Kindle books | Add at launch |
| Play | itch.io games, iOS apps | Add at launch |

- Show a section only once it has something in it. Until then, at most one line saying "things to read and play are on the way".
- Don't assume items share a world. Games and some ambience stand on their own, so don't use "the worlds of Middle Watch" framing.

## Voice: describe the moment, not the maker

Each item is described by when someone would use it.

- Audio stories: *for an evening in, a long drive or the walk home.*
- Ambience: *for falling asleep, settling into work or a slow morning.*
- Later, books: *for a quiet afternoon.* Games: *for a ten-minute break.*

Clear, warm and short. No purple prose, archaic voice or night-watch metaphors in every line.

## About Middle Watch and AI

Don't frame the brand as a one-person studio. Don't say "I write and narrate everything" or anything else that implies hand-made production. Use one plain line in the About section:

> Middle Watch is a small studio that makes stories, sound and games using modern tools, including AI, with a lot of human direction.

It gets no headline treatment, and the brand should never deny using AI.

## Headline and taglines

Current headline:

> **Something for every quiet hour.**
> Stories to listen to, soundscapes to settle into, and more on the way.

The social bios in `brand/launch-checklist.md` already use **"Somewhere else, for a while."** Pick one tagline for the site and the bios before launch (tracked in `clean-up.md`).

Alternatives:

- Somewhere else, for a while. (current bio tagline)
- Things to listen to, read and play.
- For winding down, settling in and wandering off.
- Stories, soundscapes, books and games, made with care.
- An independent label for stories, sound and small games.

## The name

The middle watch is the nautical watch from midnight to 4am. At most, one footnote line on the site explains it. Don't build the design around it.
