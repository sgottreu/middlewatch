# Middle Watch Sci-Fi Series Bible (Working Draft)

Working title: North Star
Status: Early worldbuilding. Structure mirrors the Amberlight and Broken Edges bibles.

## Premise

Cozy, frontier-flavoured sci-fi. Juniper, a young independent courier, flies her late mother's delivery circuit between a home station, a farming planet, a research moon and an asteroid mining base, with her old beagle Captain aboard. Each delivery is a self-contained story.

## Canon

- Tone is cozy. The setting feels frontier rather than corporate: independent settlements and claims, people relying on one another.
- Juniper is an independent courier in her mid-twenties.
- She took over the family route from her mother, who has died.
- Her father is a miner at Okafor. He still works, and she sees him occasionally when her route passes by.
- Captain is an old beagle who flies with her.
- Her ship is the North Star. Her father named it after her mother.

## Cast

- **Juniper**: independent courier, mid-twenties. Runs her mother's old route.
- **Captain**: old beagle, ship's companion.
- **Juniper's father**: miner at Okafor. Unnamed.
- **Juniper's mother**: deceased; ran the route before Juniper. Unnamed.

## Setting

### The Circuit

- **Dorsey's**: Juniper's home station. A mix of busy hub and shabby, much-loved old station. It has a dry official name nobody uses; everyone calls it Dorsey's, after the founder.
- **The farming planet**: unnamed. Not yet developed.
- **The research station**: on a moon. Unnamed. Not yet developed.
- **Okafor ("Okies")**: mining base on a very large asteroid with a stable orbit. Founded by the Okafor family and named after them. Crews mine the surrounding asteroids.

The research moon and Okafor are separate bodies.

### The farming planet (working ideas, not yet canon)

Blends sci-fi, western and dieselpunk: frontier homesteads, Dust Bowl grit and big old machines.

- A Dust Bowl-style world in mid-terraform. The soil is still "taking," so every harvest feels earned.
- Big diesel-style crawler tractors and grain haulers, older and passed down through families.
- A grange hall or co-op is the town's heart: dances, auctions and a radio shack.
- A local radio host whose broadcasts Juniper picks up on approach.
- To the farmers Juniper is the stagecoach: she brings parts, seed stock, mail and news.

#### Fuel economy

- The co-op runs its own fuel shed and makes canola biodiesel for the tractors: screw press, settling tanks, reactor tank, lye and methanol, heat.
- Canola fuel resists gelling, which matters in harsh winters. Press cake feeds the livestock; glycerin goes to soap and compost.
- They can't make everything. Methanol, pure catalyst (lye) and spare press and pump parts are imported, so they are Juniper's regular cargo. Homemade lye varies batch to batch, and inconsistent catalyst means bad fuel.
- Self-sufficiency options a family might pride itself on: a homemade-ethanol still (works in place of methanol), a wood-alcohol retort, a straw gasifier, a manure biogas digester, an ash-and-lime lye barrel with a kiln, or a wind- or solar-powered chlor-alkali cell.

## Story Ideas

- A seed or methanol shipment that has to arrive before planting.
- A stubborn old-timer swears by his homemade ethanol while the co-op waits on Juniper's methanol drums.
- A failing water pump or press, and the part only Juniper can bring.
- A family deciding whether to sell their claim.
- A harvest festival at the grange.
