## One-paragraph summary

Twenty years ago, a Cataclysm nobody fully understands broke the world into
isolated regions. There are drowned lowlands, a plateau city cut off from
everyone, a chiming forest, a glowing marsh, and lava fields worked by miners.
Life is mostly late medieval, and magic is just emerging through alchemy and
craft. The stories follow the mappers and chroniclers documenting this changed
world, each tale standing alone. Their recurring dilemma is choosing between
paid trade contracts and honest records, while the deeper mystery of what
really happened stays unresolved.

> A hand-drawn fantasy map in the style of an old surveyor's chart, in ink and
> watercolour on aged parchment, with muted earthy colours and no modern
> elements.
>
> Layout, north at the top:
> - Far north, across the whole width: "The Stillwinter", a frozen region of
>   snowy mountains and pine forest.
> - Just south of the Stillwinter, slightly left of centre: "The Shelf", a
>   tall, isolated plateau with sheer cliffs and only thin, winding paths down.
> - The large centre and west: "The Riven Plains", old farmland split by deep
>   chasms and crossed by makeshift bridges.
> - In the middle of the plains: "Tollgate", a small, clearly drawn market town
>   where many roads meet.
> - North-east: "The Glasswood", a pale, glinting forest.
> - Directly south of the Glasswood, in the east: "The Hollowmere", a
>   low-lying marsh that glows faintly green, with streams flowing into it from
>   the forest above and the lava fields below.
> - South-east, directly south of the Hollowmere: "The Ashfall Marches", dark,
>   cooled lava fields with steam vents and small mining camps.
> - South-west: "The Sunken Reach", flooded lowlands with drowned towns,
>   rooftops just breaking the water, and small boats.
> - South-centre, between the Sunken Reach and the Ashfall Marches: "The
>   Southern Teeth", a jagged mountain range with a few narrow passes and small
>   villages.
>
> Give the map ragged, unfinished edges with blank, unsurveyed parchment around
> the borders. Add a compass rose and a title cartouche reading "Mapping the
> Broken Edges". Label every region with exactly the names in quotes, and add
> no scale bar.
